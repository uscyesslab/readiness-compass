Partnership Readiness Compass

Carolinas School Behavioral Health TA Center (CSBHTAC)

Where is your system on the path to school partnership?

A brief self-assessment for health systems building partnerships with schools. You will respond to a short set of statements about your system's current partnership practices, and receive an instant readiness profile that highlights strengths, growth opportunities, and where coaching support can help.

This is not a pass or fail test. Every system starts somewhere, and the profile simply shows where support will be most useful.
