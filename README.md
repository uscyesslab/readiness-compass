# Partnership Readiness Compass

A brief readiness tool for health systems building school-health system partnerships. Developed by the Carolinas School Behavioral Health Technical Assistance Center (CSBHTAC), a partnership of the University of South Carolina and the Medical University of South Carolina supported by The Duke Endowment.

Live app: https://uscyesslab.github.io/readiness-compass/

## How it works

A health system team reviews 13 statements: 10 health system readiness items adapted from the School-Friendly Health System readiness checklist, plus 3 partnership items based on the Consolidated Framework for Implementation Research (CFIR) that appear when a formal district partnership exists. Each statement is rated Not yet, In progress, or In place. The result is a readiness profile across five domains (leadership and governance; partnership clarity and data sharing; resource mapping and service design; operations, staffing, and technology; awareness and continuous improvement), each rated Establishing, Developing, or Leading. The profile is not a pass or fail score. The lowest domain is the suggested starting point for coaching and technical assistance.
